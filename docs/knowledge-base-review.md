## Deliberately left out

- Result screens of the quiz ("Glückwunsch, du bist startklar!", "Du hast angegeben, einen starken Engstand zu haben."). Only the facts were taken: a first estimate in 30 seconds, what the quiz asks, and that a permanent retainer wire must be removed first.
- Medical explanations: types of malocclusion (Überbiss, Unterbiss, Kreuzbiss, Tiefbiss, offener Biss), which malocclusions can be treated, single-jaw treatment, and the "as effective as fixed braces" claim.
- Testimonials, press quotes and slogans ("Testsieger", "Spare bis zu 60%", "Gerade Zähne vom Testsieger").
- The price comparison with other providers and dentists ("Der Vergleich") and "Wie kommt unser Preis zustande?": marketing comparisons, not company policies.
- External link to www.zahnschienen.de.
